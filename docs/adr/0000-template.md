# ADR template

Use this template for architecture decision records. Copy it to a new numbered file under
`docs/adr/` and fill in the sections.

---

# ADR-####: <title>

**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces, constraints, or decisions led to this point?

## Decision

What we will do, and why this option over the alternatives considered.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, and any follow-up work or risks.
